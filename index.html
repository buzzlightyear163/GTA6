Questions

Before you ask.

What exactly do I win?

A copy of GTA 6, Standard Edition, for PS5 or Xbox Series X|S, for your own store region. Rockstar has not announced a PC version, so console only for now.

How do tickets work?

At the snapshot, the $GIVEAWAY in each wallet is valued in SOL at that moment’s price. Every full 0.3 SOL is one ticket, with no cap: 3 SOL is 10 tickets, 30 SOL is 100. Your share of tickets is your share of the holdings, so the price can’t tilt the odds.

How do I see my tickets?

Paste your wallet in “Check a wallet” above, or tap Use Phantom. The page reads your balance straight from Solana and the price from DexScreener, and shows your tickets right now. The number moves with the price until the snapshot.

When is the snapshot?

Draw #001 is the minute GTA 6 unlocks in the US: midnight Eastern on Thu 19 Nov (05:00 UTC, your time). The snapshot is taken at that minute. For later draws we post the time on X first. Hold through it and your tickets are in.

How is the winner picked?

Every wallet’s tickets are numbered in one list, which we publish with the snapshot. The winning number comes from a Solana block hash we name in advance, so nobody can choose it and anyone can check it. The winning wallet is posted on X and on the results board. The winner proves the wallet is theirs and picks PS5 or Xbox.

Who can’t win?

The dev wallet, the bonding curve and pool addresses never get tickets.

Someone DMed me saying I won.

That’s a scam. We never DM first. Winners are only posted on our X and on this page.

Is this connected to Rockstar?

No. GTA 6 is the prize, bought at the normal store price. We are not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
